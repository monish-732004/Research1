\begin{abstract}
GPU clusters suffer from \emph{resource fragmentation}: idle GPU-hours caused
by scheduling gaps, job preemptions, and heterogeneous workload shapes.
We analyse 155\,000 GPUs over six months using the Alibaba
cluster-trace-gpu-v2026 and quantify the resulting carbon waste by joining
the trace with real-time grid carbon intensity data.
We find that [X]\% of allocated GPU-time is wasted, corresponding to
[Y]\,tCO$_2$eq over the measurement period.
We further propose and evaluate CADS, a Carbon-Aware Deferral Scheduler that
reduces emissions by [Z]\% with less than [W]\% increase in job completion
time, requiring no hardware changes.
\end{abstract}
